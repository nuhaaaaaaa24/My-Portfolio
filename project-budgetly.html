About the Project

Budgetly is a smart budgeting app developed as a personal project. The app helps users track their expenses and manage their finances effectively.

The app features a user-friendly interface for logging expenses, categorizing spending, and visualizing financial data through charts and graphs. It also includes budgeting tools that allow users to set financial goals and receive insights on their spending habits.

Built with a focus on simplicity and usability, Budgetly aims to empower users to take control of their finances and make informed decisions about their spending.
